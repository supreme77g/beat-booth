How it works

1. Upload a beat

Producers upload an MP3 or WAV in the Studio and get a private link.

2. Artist records

The artist opens the link, records, overdubs ad-libs and doubles, and punches in fixes. The beat file is never handed over.

3. Verse comes back

One tap sends the full song (MP3) and every take (WAV, lined up from 0:00) to the producer, with an email alert.

Questions

Do artists get my beat file?

No. The beat plays inside the booth. Artists can record over it and download their own song, but they never get the raw beat to pass around.

What do I get back?

The full song as an MP3, plus each vocal take as a WAV that starts at 0:00 of the beat, so you can drop them straight into your DAW and they line up.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. After that your account goes back to Free. Nothing is deleted: links over the free limit stay as they are, but you can't add new ones past the limit.

Who handles payments?

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and merchant of record. Your card details never touch VerseBack.

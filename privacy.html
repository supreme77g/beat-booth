Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

This policy explains what VerseBack, run by ASW Studio, collects and how it's used. Short version: we collect what we need to run the service, we don't sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored encrypted by our login provider), and the producer or artist name you choose.
  • Your audio: beats you upload, songs and takes you save to your account, and recordings artists send to producers, plus titles, notes and artist names entered with them.
  • Plan details: whether you're on Free or Pro and your subscription status. Payment card details are handled by Paddle and never reach us.
  • Basic technical data: like your IP address and browser type, which our hosting providers log to keep the service secure and working.

On your device

The booth saves your recordings in your browser's storage so you can pick up where you left off. That stays on your device unless you save a song to your account or send it to a producer. We also store your sign-in session and a few settings (like your sync fix) in your browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run VerseBack: store your beats, play them for the artists you share links with, deliver recordings, and keep your songs.
  • To send emails you need: account confirmation, password reset, and alerts when an artist sends you vocals.
  • To manage your plan and answer support requests.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run VerseBack, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in
  • Netlify: website hosting
  • Resend: sending emails
  • Paddle: payments, as our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle's own privacy policy covers your payment details.
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts on our pages

When you share a beat link, the artists you send it to can hear the beat and see its title and your producer name. When an artist sends vocals, the producer sees the recording, artist name and note. We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete a beat, song or recording, it's removed from storage. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except what we must keep by law (for example, billing records Paddle holds).

Your choices and rights

You can see, change and delete your beats, songs and recordings in your Studio. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account, email support@verseback.com. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA; contact us and we will help.

Kids

VerseBack isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell account holders before it takes effect.

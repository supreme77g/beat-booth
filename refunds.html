Refund Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

We want you to be happy with VerseBack Pro. If it isn't right for you, here's how refunds work.

14-day refund

You can get a full refund within 14 days of any VerseBack Pro payment, monthly or yearly, first payment or renewal. No questions asked.

How to ask

  • Email support@verseback.com with the email address on your account, or
  • Contact Paddle, our reseller, through paddle.net using the link in your receipt email.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Depending on your bank it can take 5 to 10 business days to show up.

After 14 days

Payments older than 14 days aren't refunded, but you can cancel anytime from your Studio so you aren't charged again. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

Cancelling

Cancelling stops future renewals. When Pro ends, your account goes back to the free plan. Your beats, songs and recordings aren't deleted.

Who processes refunds

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle handles returns and refunds.
